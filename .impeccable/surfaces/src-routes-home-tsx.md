# Homepage

Scope: the Marlowby homepage (`/`). Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: adults 22 to 45 who love travel, food, gifts and small businesses land here to learn what Marlowby is and decide whether to subscribe. Action: Subscribe (to the plans and sign-up flow). Proof: a sample New York box made from real NYC businesses, labeled as a sample. Constraints: no invented prices, subscriber counts, testimonials or endorsements. Tier prices and city lineup are undecided, so the homepage teases them and does not state them.

Memorable moment: New York footage that dims into the serif line "Imagine you could have a whole city at home. That's Marlowby.", then dissolves into a live 3D box that opens as you scroll through a pinned stage.

Unresolved: tier prices, which cities, logo, the other six pages.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A city you can hold. The page stages one object, the month's box, the way a film stages its star. It refuses the stock subscription page: a lifestyle photo of an open box over three equal icon cards.

OWN-WORLD: Night-city footage gives way to a deep teal stage (#0E4A47), cream type (#F3E9D7), a coral field (#E8664E) for How it works, and coral otherwise only for the action and the city's mark (coral-ink #B8432E on cream). A huge expanded grotesque (Archivo, wide axis) for the wordmark and headings, Literata for the voice lines, DM Mono only for real data such as coordinates and dates. Kraft box rendered in CSS 3D with flat faces.

STORY: The visitor feels the city, hears the promise, sees exactly what arrives (a sample New York box with real makers), learns the three steps, and subscribes.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed muted New York footage under the nav (wordmark left, links and a coral Subscribe right). After about 2.4 seconds the footage dims and the serif line resolves word by word; "That's Marlowby." lands last in coral. The stage is pinned: scrolling does not move the page but dissolves the footage into a live 3D Marlowby box (Blender model, three.js) that turns, opens and closes through three text beats, in the manner of oryzo.ai. A scroll cue and a pause control sit at the bottom; a progress rail runs down the right edge.

FORM: The category standard (standing exit), raised to oryzo.ai's craft and motion (colors are the team's own teal, coral and cream). seed:739f609aba674532

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
