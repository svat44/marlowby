# Homepage

Scope: the Marlowby homepage (`/`). Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: adults 22 to 45 who love travel, food, gifts and small businesses land here to learn what Marlowby is and decide whether to subscribe. Action: Subscribe (to the plans and sign-up flow). Proof: a sample New York box made from real NYC businesses, labeled as a sample. Constraints: no invented prices, subscriber counts, testimonials or endorsements. Tier prices and city lineup are undecided, so the homepage teases them and does not state them.

Memorable moment: New York night footage that dims into the serif line "Imagine you could have a whole city at home. That's Marlowby.", then a box that opens as you scroll.

Unresolved: tier prices, which cities, logo, the other six pages.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A city you can hold. The page stages one object, the month's box, the way a film stages its star. It refuses the stock subscription page: a lifestyle photo of an open box over three equal icon cards.

OWN-WORLD: Night-city footage gives way to a dark brown-black stage (#14100C), cream type (#FFEDD7), olive (#445231) for the calm passages, and burnt orange (#DC5000) only for the action and the city's mark. A huge expanded grotesque (Archivo, wide axis) for the wordmark and headings, Literata for the voice lines, DM Mono only for real data such as coordinates and dates. Kraft box rendered in CSS 3D with flat faces.

STORY: The visitor feels the city, hears the promise, sees exactly what arrives (a sample New York box with real makers), learns the three steps, and subscribes.

A scroll cue sits at the bottom, with a pause control for the video.
